We read every message, though it may take a few days to reply.

**Email:** [contact@guide-market.com](mailto:contact@guide-market.com)

Please get in touch for:

- **Corrections** — if something in an article is wrong or out of date, tell us which article and what needs fixing.
- **Topic suggestions** — questions about AI, money, the economy or tech you'd like explained.
- **General feedback** about the site.

We don't offer personal financial, legal or tax advice by email, and we don't accept paid guest posts.
