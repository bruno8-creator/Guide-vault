Guide Market publishes practical, plain-English guides on the subjects that shape everyday decisions: artificial intelligence, the economy, personal finance and consumer technology.

## Why this site exists

Most explanations of these topics fall into one of two camps. Either they're written for specialists and assume you already know the jargon, or they're thin summaries designed to rank in search results rather than actually help. We try to sit in between: clear enough for anyone, careful enough to be worth your time.

Every article aims to answer three questions: what is this, why does it matter to me, and what can I actually do about it?

## Who we are

Guide Market is an independent publication about how technology and money affect ordinary life. Articles are researched from primary sources where possible — official statistics, central bank publications, product documentation and published research — and are updated when things change.

We're not financial advisers, and nothing on this site is personal financial, legal or medical advice. When a topic genuinely needs a professional, we say so.

## How the site is funded

Guide Market is free to read and is supported by advertising through Google AdSense. Advertisers have no say over what we write. Read more in our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy).
